Period explorations

The smallest known object of each period, as of November 2026.

The June 2009 tables, updated with later finds from the wiki (2013–2018), the ConwayLife forum thread, and the site’s own pattern files. […] denotes a flotilla whose parts interact; pseudo-objects made of independent parts aren’t counted. Cells is the minimum population (oscillators, spaceships) or initial population (puffers, which include rakes). Periods and populations were checked by simulating each pattern, except where noted. Objects not found by Luke Bradford are credited, with a link to where they were posted. For the 2008 set in one file, see Period examples (digits).

4×2n+1−4(2n−1)-slot anchor track2n+1

An anchor track with s slots has 2s+2 cells and period 4×2k−4, where k is the smallest number for which 2k is one more or one less than a multiple of 2s+1. The last row is one family this gives; 2n-slot tracks are another, with the same periods and two more cells. Most periods have a smaller track than either family, as the rows above show.
