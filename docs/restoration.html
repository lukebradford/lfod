About this restoration

The Live Free or Die Universe had two homes, and both are gone: Luke Bradford’s Verizon site (mysite.verizon.net/live_free/, 2007–2011) and the wiki at lfod.wikispaces.com (2013–2018). This site rebuilds them from the original files, the Wayback Machine, and the ConwayLife forum.

Every oscillator and spaceship in the glossary has been run in a B2/S0 simulator to confirm that its period and speed match its description. The puffers, rakes and forum patterns were checked by eye in the player. Nine glossary objects that the 2009 site showed only as pictures were decoded back into patterns from those images; they are marked “from image”.

How the pages map

  • Home, News (to September 2009), Glossary, Tables, Periods, Iterations, Challenges and Files follow the 2009 site, with its text kept as written.
  • Glossary entries marked “added wiki” come from the archived wiki. Where the wiki rewrote a 2009 entry, both versions are shown.
  • News after 2009 and the “Since then” section of Challenges were reconstructed from the forum and archived pages. Each item says where it came from.

What is lost

  • About 100 wiki pages were never archived. Many repeat 2009 glossary entries, but these names from 2013–2016 have no surviving page: collider ship, ellipsis puffer, hex rake, hippo rake, plow trailer, pocket rake, quintessential rake, wide rhino ship, zebra ship, zipper rake and big six shooter stack, plus the Iteration guides and the p1–p19 period pages. Some of these objects survive in forum posts, just without their names attached. The buffalo, hippo, horned and fox ships, the horned rake and the sawtooth also had pages that were never archived; they have been restored from the forum and from Luke’s private messages.
  • The updated collection and the wiki exports. The wiki’s 2014 HTML and PDF exports (lfod_20140922, lfod_20140929) and any collection newer than October 2013 are lost.
  • The wiki after March 2018, and all page histories.
  • Verizon site images on the Wayback Machine. None were captured, but the local copies survive and are used here.

Where more might be found

  • Anyone who downloaded the 2014 wiki export or a later collection. If that’s you, please get in touch.
