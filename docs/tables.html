Legend

Discovery method (Disc)

N – Natural · C – Constructed · ET – Explosion tracking · WLS – WinLifeSearch program · J.S. – Jason Summers’ Seeds collection · D.E. – David Eppstein · J.B. – Josh Ball · PSF – Pre-spark-find program

Symmetry (Sym)

O – Eight-way · D – Two-way diagonal · / – Four-way diagonal · S – Two-way rotational · M – Two-way mirror · I – Four-way mirror · G – Glide symmetric

Engine resultant (ER)

EaP – Engine as phase · EaI – Engine as initial state · SER – Strictly engine resultant · LER – Loosely engine resultant

Engine address (EA)

Numerals correspond to a wing of length n.
_ nose with both adjacent wings attached · – nose with both adjacent wings floating · / nose with left wing attached and right wing floating · \ nose with left wing floating and right wing attached · & a wing not adjacent to a nose, but appended to another wing as in a dot puffer · . space of one pixel, with nose on other side at the same vertical location · , space of one pixel, with nose on other side displaced down one pixel · ‘ space of one pixel, with nose on other side displaced up one pixel · * additional engine element that cannot be described in EA notation

Pre-spark mechanism

mechanism 1 – next generation of a particular mechanism · (n) – unnamed mechanism which is the smallest pre-spark of duration n · [n] – unnamed mechanism which is not the smallest pre-spark of duration n, but used in other mechanisms · mechanism + mechanism – first mechanism sets off a chain reaction in the second · HSn – hot sand mechanism of n cells
