Rule 110 unit cell

FWKnightship’s Rule 110 emulator in Live Free or Die, posted on 20 October 2020. Rule 110 is a one-dimensional cellular automaton that is known to be universal, so emulating it showed that Live Free or Die is Turing-complete.

The torus is 4320 × 7200 and holds six unit cells, each 720 cells wide. Each unit cell stores its Rule 110 state in a lone dot at the top (circled): a live dot means 1. Fleets of lightspeed ships sweep down the torus, read the dots, and rewrite them. Each sweep takes 7200 generations, the height of the torus, and computes one Rule 110 generation.

Simulated on your GPU. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan. The panel shows the six dots, read every 7200 generations. Forum post · Download RLE (also distributed with Golly).
