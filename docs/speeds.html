Speed explorations

The smallest known object of each speed, as of November 2026.

Speeds are orthogonal unless marked diagonal; c is one cell per generation. Cells is the minimum population (spaceships) or initial population (puffers and rakes), checked by simulating each pattern. As on the period tables, puffers include rakes, and objects not found by Luke Bradford are credited, with a link to where they were posted or announced where one survives. For speeds not yet reached, see Challenges.
