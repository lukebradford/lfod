Challenges

Patterns not yet known in Live Free or Die, as of November 2026.

Compiled from the period tables, the wiki’s last list of challenges (April 2015), and the ConwayLife forum thread.

  • Objects of period 11, 13, 14, 15, 17, 21, 22 and 23, and of most higher periods not in the period tables
  • Oscillators of period 7, 9, 10, 11, 13 to 18, or 20 to 27
  • Spaceships of period 7, 9, 11, 13, 14, 15, 17, 18 or 19. Partial p9 lightspeed ships were posted to the forum in 2021, but none was completed.
  • Puffers and rakes of period 11, 13, 14, 15, 17 or 19
  • Spaceships of speeds other than c, c/2, 2c/5, c/3, c/4 and c/5 orthogonally, and c/4 diagonally
  • Puffers and rakes slower than c, other than at c/2 and c/5
  • Knightships: spaceships or puffers that travel obliquely. A (2,1)c/5 front end was posted in 2018, but never completed.
  • A reusable reflector for moons, ideally a stable one. The only known moon reflectors destroy themselves.
  • Guns for any spaceship other than the moon, and moon guns of periods other than 4 and 5
  • Signal circuitry analogous to Herschel conduits in Conway’s Life
  • Spacefillers, other than the light-speed expansion of chaos
  • Replicators
  • Stabilizations for wicks and oscillator components that work the same way in Seeds (B2/S)

History

The 2008 site kept a list of open problems, promising “eternal gratitude, naming rights, and a place of glory on this web site” to anyone who solved one. The wiki carried it on from 2013 and in July 2014 added cash prizes: $25 for a spaceship of a new speed, $25 for any moon reflector and $50 for a stable one. Michael Simkin’s c/3 wasp ship won the speed prize in October 2014. Many of the 2008 challenges have since been met, among them guns (2012), c/3 and c/4 spaceships, period 10 (2012), p2 puffers (2014), breeders (2015), and a Rule 110 unit cell showing that the rule is universal (2020). Neither offer stands today.
